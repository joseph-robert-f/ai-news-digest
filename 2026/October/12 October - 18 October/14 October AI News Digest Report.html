Models12 Oct

Meta releases Muse Wonder, its first frontier model

Why it matters: Meta is now a paid, closed-weight API competitor; test it on price, but treat its benchmark claims as vendor-reported.

Detail & sources

Meta’s Superintelligence Lab released Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full. Reports give API pricing of $4 per million input tokens and $20 per million output tokens, and a 1M-token context window. Wonder Full is limited to Muse Code subscribers. Meta says performance is comparable to rivals’ top models. Artificial Analysis’s index reportedly scores Wonder Full at 56 and Wonder at 52, against 58 for Claude Opus 5.5. The models are closed-weight, a break from Meta’s open-source history.
