Policy14 Oct

Eight US states sue OpenAI over ChatGPT harm to young users

Why it matters: this is the largest coordinated state action against an AI lab, and it lands as OpenAI prepares to list.

Detail & sources

Arkansas, Arizona, Idaho, Iowa, Kansas, Montana, Nebraska and West Virginia filed on 14 October. They allege deceptive safety claims and public nuisance, and seek penalties plus orders that include barring self-improving models not shown to be aligned. Florida filed the first such suit in June. OpenAI had not responded at the time of reporting. Filing format differs by report: some say separate state-court filings.

Security13 Oct

OpenAI engineer charged over hidden recorder in executive conference room

Why it matters: frontier labs are now insider-risk targets, and this case is a criminal matter, not a trade-secret story.

Detail & sources

The Justice Department charged Abdulrahman Alfozan, 35, with intercepting oral communications. Staff found a device behind a TV on 30 September; OpenAI told the FBI and fired him. The maximum penalty is five years. A detention hearing is set for 19 October. No link to the Apple trade-secret suit is reported, and he is presumed innocent. This updates the earlier report that an employee hid a device.
