Policy30 Oct

Four AI giants reportedly agree on one robots.txt opt-out for AI training

Why it matters: Publishers may soon block training with a single rule, but the pledge is voluntary and the dates are far apart.

Detail & sources

Reports say OpenAI, Google, Microsoft and Anthropic will back one shared robots.txt token. Anthropic says it has started; OpenAI and Microsoft point to coming months; Google is described as early 2027. No law or regulator is involved. Source quality is mixed: these are secondary outlets, and an official vendor post was not found. Treat the details as unconfirmed. Cloudflare separately says Microsoft support for a robots.txt no-training preference is targeted for early 2027.
