US bars DeepSeek products from all civilian federal agencies

Why it matters: Any vendor that embeds DeepSeek models may now be excluded from federal work, so government contractors should audit their model supply chains.

Detail & sources

A Department of Homeland Security notice, due in the Federal Register on 20 October, bars civilian agencies from using or buying DeepSeek products, including third-party products built on DeepSeek implementations; agencies can request exceptions. It is a procurement rule, not an export control, and does not cover private firms. Outlets disagree on the date of the underlying order (June 26 versus July 24). China’s foreign ministry objected.

PHP2Shell: critical PHP flaws hit with exploit attempts before patches

Why it matters: Patch PHP now; a large share of the web runs it, and exploit attempts began before fixes shipped.

Detail & sources

PHP released emergency fixes for two unauthenticated remote-code-execution bugs (CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619, CVSS 9.2) in versions 8.5.12, 8.4.27, 8.3.36 and 8.2.35, with backports for older lines. Palo Alto Networks counted more than 22.6 million potentially exposed instances. Apache with mod_php is at highest risk. WatchTowr reported attempts but no confirmed successes at the time. Some details remain unverified.
