Infrastructure17 Aug

Nvidia backs up to $100B of OpenAI's Ohio buildout inside a $500B Wall Street financing web

Why it matters: Nvidia is no longer just selling GPUs into the AI buildout — it's underwriting it, using its own chips as loan collateral through special-purpose vehicles built with Wall Street. That circularity concentrates enormous credit risk in one company's balance sheet and makes the entire buildout's financing sensitive to Nvidia's own valuation.

Detail & sources

Nvidia and OpenAI are negotiating up to $100B in credit support and as much as $350B in GPU financing for a 10-gigawatt data-center campus at PORTS-Pike in Pike County, Ohio, built and managed by SB Energy under a 20-year lease. Nvidia is investing $1.5B directly in SB Energy alongside SoftBank and OpenAI. The Ohio deal sits inside a broader platform announced with Apollo, Blackstone, BlackRock, Brookfield, Goldman Sachs and KKR to raise $500B in third-party capital for AI data centers globally, structured as private bonds issued by special-purpose entities using compute capacity as collateral.

Anthropic posts its first-ever operating profit on $11.5B+ Q2 revenue

Why it matters: This is the clearest proof yet that a frontier lab can run a real business, not just raise capital against a growth story — and it hands Anthropic a materially stronger pitch than OpenAI's for their competing IPOs this fall, at a moment when investors are increasingly asking AI labs to show margin, not just top-line growth.

Detail & sources

Preliminary figures put Q2 revenue above $11.5B, up from $4.73B in Q1 and $787M a year earlier, alongside positive adjusted operating income for the first time — a metric that includes model-training costs but excludes stock-based compensation. Growth was driven largely by enterprise adoption of Claude Code and coding-agent products. The numbers are preliminary and could still move before Anthropic's formal reporting.

Stripe finalizes its $7B+ acquisition of OpenRouter

Why it matters: A payments company now owns the neutral routing layer sitting in front of 400+ models from every major lab, for roughly 8 million developers — a bet that model choice, not any single model, is the durable infrastructure layer, and a sign that AI-gateway startups are becoming acquisition targets rather than independent platforms.

Detail & sources

The price is a 5.4x markup over OpenRouter's $1.3B Series B valuation from just three months earlier in May. OpenRouter lets developers route requests across models from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta and DeepSeek based on cost, latency and task fit; Stripe already processed its payments, giving it visibility into the startup's growth before the deal.

Meta's youth-safety trial opens, states seeking up to $200B

Why it matters: This is the largest social-media harms case yet to reach trial, testing whether internal design decisions — not just moderation failures — can carry multibillion-dollar liability. It lands the same week AI capex and a ~700% DRAM price surge crushed Meta's free cash flow to $784M, squeezing the company financially and legally at once.

Detail & sources

A coalition of 29 state attorneys general, led by California's Rob Bonta, opened arguments Tuesday in federal court before Judge Yvonne Gonzalez Rogers, alleging Meta knowingly designed addictive features for minors and collected data on under-13 users without consent. States have floated theoretical damages as high as $1.4T, with attorneys signaling roughly $200B as more realistic, plus platform-design changes. Separately, Meta raised its 2026 capex floor to $130B (ceiling $145B) after Q2 capex hit $31.1B, and a DRAM price spike helped push free cash flow down to $784M, freezing buybacks for a third straight quarter.

Developer Platforms17 Aug

Google retires Imagen 4 API endpoints, forcing a pricier migration

Why it matters: This isn't a drop-in model swap — the whole calling convention changes, and the replacement costs more per image for many workloads. Any team that hadn't already migrated has a broken integration as of today, a reminder that model retirements now carry real engineering and budget costs on tight timelines.

Detail & sources

Google shut down the imagen-4.0-generate-001, imagen-4.0-ultra-generate-001 and imagen-4.0-fast-generate-001 endpoints on August 17, directing developers to gemini-3.1-flash-image. The old generate_images() method is gone entirely; image generation now runs through generate_content(), which accepts more input types and bills by output resolution. A 1K replacement image now costs $0.067, versus $0.02–$0.06 under Imagen 4.

Developer Platforms12–13 Aug

Agent Plugins 1.0 goes GA across Copilot, Cursor and ChatGPT

Why it matters: A cross-vendor standard for packaging agent skills and MCP servers into one installable plugin means developers can finally build an integration once and ship it to multiple agent clients, instead of maintaining separate versions per vendor — a real step toward commoditizing the agent-tooling layer that every lab has been racing to lock down.

Detail & sources

Agent Plugins 1.0, co-published by AWS, Anysphere, Microsoft, OpenAI, Vercel and Google, reached general availability in VS Code, GitHub Copilot CLI, the Copilot SDK and the Copilot app. It bundles a skill and an MCP server into one distributable plugin governed independently of any single vendor; Copilot CLI also picked up subagent management, queued prompts, headless autopilot and a /rewind command in the same release.

Security17 Aug

Unpatched Unisoc modem flaw gives full Android kernel access via a video call

Why it matters: An attacker with a private cellular network can fully compromise a phone the moment its owner answers a video call, no user action beyond that required — and with the chipmaker unresponsive to disclosure for months, hundreds of millions of budget Android devices have no available fix.

Detail & sources

SSD Secure Disclosure published a two-stage exploit chain against Unisoc modem firmware on August 17, building on a March 2026 disclosure of remote code execution via a malformed SIP video call. The flaw (an improper isolation of shared SoC resources, CWE-1189) lets an attacker disable modem memory protections and pivot into Android kernel memory once a targeted video call is answered. Affected devices demonstrated or listed include the Realme C33, Xiaomi Redmi A5 and Motorola E13; researchers said Unisoc has not responded to outreach through email or LinkedIn.
