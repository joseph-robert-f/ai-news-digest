Defense1 Sep

Pentagon adds ChatGPT and Grok to its military AI portal, keeps pressing to purge Claude

Why it matters: The Defense Department is diversifying its sanctioned AI stack rather than pausing to reconsider Anthropic, signaling the September 30 removal deadline holds even after a federal judge called the blacklist "illegal and baseless."

Detail & sources

GenAI.mil — the DoD's secure portal for commercial frontier models — now bundles OpenAI's "ChatGPT Mil" and Starshield/xAI's "Grok for Government" alongside Google's Gemini, which launched on the platform in December. More than 1.7 million of the department's 3 million personnel have already onboarded. Separately, reporting indicates the Air Force pushed contractors to purge Anthropic products by September 1, with a department-wide deadline of September 29–30, even though a California court found the designation illegally retaliatory. Anthropic's suit to overturn the blacklist continues.

Business31 Aug

OpenAI's ChatGPT ad business crosses $1B annualized revenue — in 200 days

Why it matters: Ads are becoming a real diversification leg for OpenAI's revenue story ahead of an expected IPO, not a side experiment — the company is now targeting roughly $40B in annualized revenue, about double its end-of-2025 run rate.

Detail & sources

OpenAI's self-serve ad platform, which began testing in the U.S. in February, has expanded to more than 40 countries and this week opened to marketers in India, Europe, the Middle East, and North Africa. Ads run only for free-tier and Go-subscription users, are labeled, and OpenAI says are kept separate from how ChatGPT generates answers. The company is targeting $2.5B in ad revenue this year.

Google ships Gemini 3.1 Pro to Vertex AI and Gemini Enterprise

Why it matters: Google is pitching 3.1 Pro as a meaningfully cheaper, more reliable reasoning upgrade rather than a new flagship — fewer output tokens for comparable or better results is a direct pitch at enterprise inference cost, not just benchmark bragging rights.

Detail & sources

Gemini 3.1 Pro is available in preview via Vertex AI, Gemini Enterprise, the Gemini API in Google AI Studio, Android Studio, Google Antigravity, and Gemini CLI. Google reports up to 15% improvement over the best Gemini 3 Pro Preview runs and a verified 77.1% on ARC-AGI-2, more than double Gemini 3 Pro's reasoning score on that benchmark.
