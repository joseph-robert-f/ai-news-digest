Anthropic sets an IPO range of $70–$80 per share

Why it matters: The listing is the first public price for a frontier lab; valuation talk has already softened, so treat headline figures as moving.

Detail & sources

One report says the expected valuation fell from about $2T to about $1.41T, and Fortune links market jitters partly to instability at Nvidia. Markets10 Nov

Anthropic adds a $26B private placement from its own suppliers

Why it matters: Chip, cloud and memory vendors are buying stock in a large customer, which ties their revenue to its IPO outcome.

Detail & sources

Per the S-1/A as reported: Nvidia and Google $10B each, AMD $2.5B, Broadcom, Micron and Microsoft $1B each, and TSMC $500M. Together with the IPO, the raise would be roughly $87B–$95B. Reuters reported earlier that Nvidia was weighing up to $10B of IPO backing. Decision point for buyers of AI infrastructure: supplier equity stakes can blur pricing and concentration risk.

CrowdStrike ships an AI Kill Switch and buys Fiddler AI and Dreadnode

Why it matters: Agent governance is moving from policy to enforcement: security teams can now stop a running agent, cut its network access and block relaunch.

Detail & sources

Announced 3 November at Fal.Con Europe. The Kill Switch in Falcon Guardian first acts at the AI Gateway; endpoint termination is listed as coming soon. CrowdStrike signed deals for Fiddler AI (agent observability) and Dreadnode (automated AI testing); prices were not disclosed and closing is expected in its fiscal Q4 2027. Analysts note that stopping a process does not undo actions the agent already took. Sources disagree on whether the Kill Switch is already generally available. This is older than 24 hours; it is included because it is the clearest enterprise agent-control launch this month and prior digests did not cover it.

RuntimeWire says it found an unreleased “Introducing ChatGPT Wallet” screen in the Windows ChatGPT/Codex app bundle. OpenAI has not announced a wallet.
