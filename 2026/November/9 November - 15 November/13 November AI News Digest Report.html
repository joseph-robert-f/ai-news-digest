Company12 Nov

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang resigns; CFO Colette Kress steps in as interim

Why it matters: The AI supply chain's most important leader is gone with no named successor, so expect partner and customer uncertainty until the board picks one.

Detail & sources

New since the 12 November report of a board probe: Nvidia's 8-K says Huang resigned, and Kress is interim CEO while remaining CFO. Reports say Huang also left the board and the president role, and Harvey Jones became the first board chairman. The filing gives no reason. Press reports link the exit to allegations about personal relationships and a suspected China espionage link; these are unconfirmed by the company. Successor names (Tim Cook, Ian Buck and others) are speculation.
