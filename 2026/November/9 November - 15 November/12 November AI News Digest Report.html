Anthropic ships Claude Fable 5.2 and a vetted-only Mythos 5.2

Why it matters: Frontier labs now sell the same model in two tiers; cyber and bio capability goes only to verified buyers, so security and life-science teams need to apply for access.

Detail & sources

Fable 5.2 is available to all users on all platforms, including AWS, Google Cloud and Azure, at $8 per million input and $40 per million output tokens (fast mode: $16/$80). Mythos 5.2 is the same underlying model without Fable's cyber and biology safeguards. It is limited to organizations in Anthropic's Cyber and Life Sciences Verification Programs. Anthropic reports 89.6% on its CVE Rediscovery evaluation, up from 78.3% for Mythos 5.1. These are company-reported figures; independent testing is not yet published.
