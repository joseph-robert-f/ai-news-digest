Chips9 Nov

Google unveils TPU 8tx for confidential AI and TPU 8i Max for long context

Why it matters: Hardware-level encryption gives security-bound buyers (finance, health, government) a reason to run sensitive inference on Google silicon instead of waiting for on-prem GPUs.

Detail & sources

TPU 8tx uses the TPU 8t silicon with hardware encryption and AMD EPYC CPUs; Google claims up to 2x lower latency than TPU 8t with near-zero penalty, and may let select customers deploy it in their own data centers. TPU 8i Max pairs two TPU 8i chips for double the compute, SRAM and HBM bandwidth and 384 GB of HBM. Performance figures are Google's own and not independently verified. The 8t/8i generation itself was first shown in April 2026 (older background).

Security9 Nov

Fireworks AI says its October breach reached customer secrets, datasets and partial model weights

Why it matters: If you fine-tune or host on a third-party inference platform, rotate keys now and ask the vendor what stored training data and checkpoints it holds.

Detail & sources

Fireworks first detected the intrusion on 6 October and first said only evaluation-job environment variables were exposed. Its 9 November update says attackers reached account and billing information and, for a limited group of customers, stored secrets, datasets and incomplete model weights. Fireworks says it found no impact to Inference, the Training SDK or Nexus (zero data retention by default) and that it does not store card numbers. Outside coverage varies on whether personal data was involved; the vendor bulletin is the authority. Update: this widens the October disclosure.

Enterprise9 Nov

OpenAI sells ChatGPT Enterprise through AWS Marketplace

Why it matters: AWS-committed buyers can now fund ChatGPT from existing cloud commitments, which removes a procurement barrier and sharpens the cloud-channel fight with Microsoft and Anthropic.

Detail & sources

OpenAI is the seller; pricing is by private offer. The bundle covers ChatGPT, ChatGPT Work and Codex, and eligible customers can add the Healthcare and Financial Services editions. Buying through Marketplace does not guarantee that all customer data stays on AWS infrastructure. Reporting notes that credit-to-invoice mechanics and AWS's revenue share were not disclosed. This builds on OpenAI models arriving on Amazon Bedrock earlier in 2026 (background).
