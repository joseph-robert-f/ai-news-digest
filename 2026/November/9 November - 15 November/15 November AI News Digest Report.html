TSMC approves up to $1B in Anthropic and OpenAI stakes

Why it matters: The chip foundry is moving from supplier to shareholder, and the Anthropic stake is priced at the IPO, so it is a direct read on supplier confidence.

Detail & sources

Anthropic launched its roadshow the same day: 825M Class A shares, a $70–$78 range and a Nasdaq listing as “ANT”. Security6–9 Nov

Notion’s own advisory: AI chats may be exposed via Fireworks AI

Why it matters: Your AI vendor’s vendor is now part of your breach surface; ask suppliers which inference hosts they use and whether prompts are logged.

Detail & sources

Notion says Fireworks AI told it on 6 November of an incident that may affect fewer than 1% of workspaces. The data came from the Insights feature. Notion says most records cannot identify the speaker or workspace, but advises treating the data as exposed and rotating any credentials pasted into Notion AI chats. It disabled Insights and stopped serving open-weight models through Fireworks. This adds Notion’s side to the Fireworks breach in the 11 November digest. The exact number of affected workspaces is not confirmed.

Agents11 Nov

United bans unauthorized third-party AI agents from booking travel

Why it matters: Airlines are choosing approved integrations over open agent access, so any agent-commerce plan needs a direct partnership, not scraping.

Detail & sources

United says its contract of carriage now bars AI agents from making, changing or servicing tickets, even when a customer supervises. Bookings made that way can be cancelled without notice. It cites security, privacy and regulatory risk, and says it has no third-party agent partnerships today. Southwest has opened inventory to ChatGPT users, and Delta says it supports agents that can be authenticated. Whether United can detect agent bookings made through aggregators is disputed. Extends the 12 November item on sites shutting out shopping agents.

Policy13 Nov

EU leaders hold their first AI-only summit in Dublin

Why it matters: Europe is debating dependence and sovereignty at head-of-government level; expect follow-up in 2027, not binding rules now.

Detail & sources

The 27 EU leaders met informally in Dublin with AI as the only agenda item. Topics were reducing critical dependencies, building European AI capability and infrastructure. Mistral CEO Arthur Mensch and Geoffrey Hinton were due to speak. Informal meetings take no formal decisions, and Council President Costa expects leaders to return to AI in 2027. I found no report of the outcome, only previews, so treat results as unconfirmed.

LegalOngoing (filed 18 Sep)

Antitrust suit says Anthropic, OpenAI, SpaceXAI and Google colluded to slow AI

Why it matters: Safety coordination between rivals now carries antitrust risk, which matters as labs discuss shared limits and a federal framework.

Detail & sources

Background: four paying subscribers filed Buist et al. v. Anthropic PBC et al. (N.D. Cal., 26-cv-10693) on 18 September, seeking class status, damages and an injunction. They argue a joint slowdown reduces what subscribers get; they do not object to a single company slowing down. The suit followed a September essay by Dario Amodei that Altman, Musk and Hassabis publicly backed. No ruling found.
