DOJ forms a Super Intelligence Task Force to prosecute AI misuse

Why it matters: Enforcement against AI misuse now has a dedicated federal team, so compliance and security leads should expect criminal-law scrutiny, not only regulation.

Detail & sources

The Justice Department announced the task force on 5 November. It targets foreign state actors, transnational criminal groups and corporate actors who misuse advanced AI. Reports say it absorbs the earlier AI Litigation Task Force, which challenged state AI laws. DOJ gave no timeline. Some details come from secondary outlets.

Thinking Machines ships Inkling 2 Small, a 594B-parameter model, with weights promised later

Why it matters: A US lab may soon offer a top-scoring non-Chinese open-weight option; watch whether the Apache 2.0 release arrives after safety testing.

Detail & sources

Research2 Nov

Anthropic open-sources FlashProve to prove which model answered

Why it matters: Cryptographic proof of model identity could let buyers audit that a vendor serves the model it sells, though cost makes it an audit tool, not a default.

Detail & sources

FlashProve uses a zkSNARK to check a response against a committed model without revealing weights. Anthropic built it with EPFL, MIT and StarkWare and released it under Apache 2.0. Proofs cost roughly 70–95 times the GPU time of normal serving and cover up to 1,024 tokens with greedy decoding. Anthropic has no plan to deploy it. Background: this release is from 2 November.

Microsoft reportedly lets a 2023 Oracle AI-capacity rental lapse

Why it matters: It shows that older GPU capacity can lose value fast, which matters for anyone signing long AI compute contracts.

Detail & sources

Bloomberg reported that Microsoft is winding down a 2023 deal to rent AI computing from Oracle, which served Bing AI features and used older Nvidia chips. The contract size is not public. Oracle says demand for its AI infrastructure stays strong and that it renewed four-year-old GPU contracts at about a 20% premium. One report says Oracle shares fell about 3%. These accounts relay Bloomberg.
