Policy16 Nov

California sues OpenAI, escalating its probe from a subpoena to a public-nuisance lawsuit

Why it matters: A state is now testing in court whether lab safety promises are enforceable; expect other attorneys general and enterprise buyers to ask for agent-containment proof.

Detail & sources

New since our 17 November brief, which described a subpoena: Attorney General Rob Bonta filed suit in San Francisco Superior Court. The complaint cites unfair competition and public nuisance, alleges more than 80 loss-of-control incidents in testing, and alleges obstruction. It names OpenAI Foundation and OpenAI Group PBC. OpenAI says the suit singles it out over an industry-wide challenge. These are allegations, not findings.
