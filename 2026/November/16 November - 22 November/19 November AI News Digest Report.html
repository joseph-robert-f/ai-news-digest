Policy16–19 Nov

US remote-access chip rule reportedly takes effect immediately

Why it matters: Renting GPUs in third countries for Chinese customers may now need a licence, so cloud and neocloud contracts need a compliance review.

Detail & sources

Update to the 18 November item that said publication was pending: coverage now describes an interim final rule dated 16 November, “Addressing Remote Access to Advanced Semiconductors”, effective immediately. Reported scope includes licences for chips going to data centres that serve China, and added licensing for shipments to Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia, Thailand and Brazil. Details come from secondary coverage; the Federal Register text was not confirmed. Export-control lawyers have questioned whether Commerce has authority over remote access. The Remote Access Security Act passed the House 369–22 in January.
