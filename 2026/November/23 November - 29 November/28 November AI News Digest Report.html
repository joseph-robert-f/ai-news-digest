Black Friday weekend is the first big test of AI-driven shopping traffic

Why it matters: Retail and marketing teams should watch Adobe’s final numbers: they will show whether AI assistants are sending buyers or only browsers.

Detail & sources

Huawei reportedly asks some customers to source their own HBM for Ascend 960DT

Why it matters: US export controls keep constraining China’s AI compute supply; memory, not logic, is the bottleneck to watch.

Detail & sources

The Information reported that Huawei told some customers its next flagship chip, due in Q1 2027, will run largely on Samsung and SK hynix memory and that customers should source it themselves. Outlets relaying the report did not independently verify it, and Huawei has not confirmed it. SK hynix says it has not supplied Huawei since US restrictions took effect.

Open modelsBackground · 7 Nov

Nous Research releases Arete-27B, a Qwen fine-tune aimed at state-aligned answers

Why it matters: If you deploy open Chinese-origin models, this is a cheap, auditable recipe for reducing political skew — but the evidence is the vendor’s own.

Detail & sources

Arete-27B is Qwen3.8-27B after supervised fine-tuning only (about 52.8M tokens), with Thinking Machines’ Inkling as teacher. On Nous’s own 803-prompt test, flagged answers fell from 22% to 0.9%. Independent testing is thin, and temperature settings differed between rows. Weights and data are on Hugging Face.

  • Let’s Data Science: Nous fine-tunes Qwen3.8-27B
  • AI Weekly: Arete-27B cuts propaganda 22% to 0.9%
