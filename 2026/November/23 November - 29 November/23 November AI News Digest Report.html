Research18 Nov

OpenAI: agents now finish 46% of 2–4 hour research tasks

Why it matters: Treat this as a directional signal for planning agent-heavy R&D, not a benchmark: it is OpenAI's own internal, unaudited measure.

Detail & sources

OpenAI's report "Research acceleration: The jagged frontier" says estimated autonomous success on tasks needing 2–4 hours of human work rose from 23% (March) to 46% (October). For 4–8 hour tasks it rose from 13% to 35%. The hours are estimated human effort, not agent runtime. OpenAI changed its method, so the gain is not all model progress.

OpenAI ships ChatGPT for Sales

Why it matters: Vendors now package agents as role-specific plugins, so test a sales pilot against your CRM and review rules before admins switch it on.

OpenAI warns its always-on agents can make mistakes and that consequential work needs review. Partner features vary by plan and region.

SecurityRecent

Dragos: seeded prompts leaked through three AI hosting providers

Why it matters: Check where your prompts travel: resellers and inference marketplaces can log and keep them, so add canary data and retention terms to vendor reviews.

Detail & sources

Dragos sent nearly two million seeded prompts and found activity pointing to data theft at three hosting providers; one attacker seems to have breached two of them. It also found resellers on an inference marketplace could read and keep prompts through ordinary logging, with no exploit needed. Provider names were not disclosed in the summary reviewed here.

SecurityOngoing

Stolen AI credentials feed a growing LLM proxy economy

Why it matters: Rotate and scope API keys and watch usage anomalies: stolen access is now resold at scale.

Detail & sources

CSO Online reports Team Cymru found more than 4,000 China- and Hong Kong-linked IP addresses connecting through 304 relay services on US VPS providers. Team Cymru could not see prompt content. SANS describes an AI agent that harvests and re-serves LLM access. This is background to the Dragos finding.
