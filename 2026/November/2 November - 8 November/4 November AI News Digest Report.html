Markets2 Nov

Marvell agrees to buy optics maker Lumentum in an all-stock deal

Why it matters: AI networking and optics are now worth acquiring at scale; expect more chip-and-interconnect consolidation and closer antitrust scrutiny.

Detail & sources

Companies announced the definitive agreement on 2 November. Lumentum holders receive 4.5 Marvell shares per share, an implied enterprise value of about $115 billion and a premium of about 23.9% to the 28 October unaffected price. Marvell holders would own about 67% of the combined company. Bloomberg first reported talks on 28 October. One internal Lumentum email cites about $100 billion; the filing figure is used here. The deal still needs closing approvals.
