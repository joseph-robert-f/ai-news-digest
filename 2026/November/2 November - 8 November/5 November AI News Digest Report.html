Nvidia reportedly tells cloud partners to keep Chinese customers under 49% of new contracts

Why it matters: If real, this extends US-aligned export control into private contract terms and reduces the offshore cloud route Chinese labs use to reach Nvidia compute.

Detail & sources

The Financial Times reported the rule on 3 November, citing anonymous sources. It reportedly covers data center operators worldwide, applies to new contracts only, and asks for end-user reporting. Nvidia has not published the rule; its public statement was that America cannot win the AI race unless its infrastructure wins worldwide. Background (4 September): Bloomberg reported DeepSeek plans to order about 160,000 Huawei Ascend 950DT chips for a Mongolian site, mainly for inference, which shows the pressure on Chinese buyers to localize. DeepSeek and Huawei have not confirmed.

Legal2 Nov

NYT and three other US papers sue Brave over AI scraping and paywall bypass

Why it matters: Publishers are moving from suing model makers to suing the search and browser layer, which could set rules for any product that grounds answers in news.

Detail & sources

Complaints filed in Manhattan allege Brave sells scraped articles to AI firms, returns near-verbatim passages in its Ask Brave feature, and offers a setting that bypasses paywalls. The Chicago Tribune, Daily News and Orlando Sentinel publishers filed a separate suit. Brave says it only indexes content as every search engine must. These are allegations, not findings. News Corp also countersued Brave in July.
