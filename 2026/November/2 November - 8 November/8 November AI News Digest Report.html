InfrastructureReported 7 Nov

Nvidia reportedly pitches Apollo on small AI data centers in old telecom buildings

Why it matters: If local bans keep spreading, siting capacity in existing powered buildings may become the faster route to new compute.

Detail & sources

The Information reports Nvidia approached Apollo about converting more than 130 buildings of Brightspeed, a telecom reportedly near bankruptcy, into small AI sites across as many as 20 states. Coverage cites roughly 1 GW of available power and Nvidia's modular DSX POD design. This is a single paywalled report with secondary pickup; neither company has confirmed it. It follows the local data-center ballot results in the 6 November brief.

OpenAI reportedly plans to buy Google TPUs; Oracle will offer TPUs from 2027

Why it matters: Nvidia's largest customers are lining up alternatives, which strengthens buyer leverage on price and supply.
