Voters back data-center limits in 40 of 41 local measures

Why it matters: siting risk is now an electoral risk; expect longer permitting and more moratoriums for AI build-outs.

Detail & sources

Platforms3 Nov

Google will cut Search for schools that turn off its AI tools

Why it matters: bundling AI with a core product invites regulatory and public backlash, and gives rivals such as DuckDuckGo an opening in education.

Detail & sources

Google says AI features and Search will be off by default for K-12 student accounts until administrators enable them. Schools that bar AI lose Search in school accounts. From early 2027 districts that allow AI choose between AI Overviews and chats, or Guided Learning. Former FTC chair Lina Khan called it coercion.
