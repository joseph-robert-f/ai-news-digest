Apple × OpenAI10 Jul

Apple sues OpenAI for trade-secret theft, turning a former partner into a litigant weeks before OpenAI's expected IPO filing

Why it matters: this is the first direct litigation between the two companies and reframes their relationship as fully adversarial just as both race into on-device and hardware AI — a discovery process could surface internal OpenAI hardware plans right as the company is laying groundwork for a public listing.

Detail & sources

Apple filed suit against OpenAI on 10 July in the Northern District of California, alleging a "coordinated campaign" in which OpenAI's hardware chief Tang Tan — a former Apple VP — and other ex-Apple staff, including engineer Chang Liu, carried confidential silicon and on-device AI designs into OpenAI's hardware push (built around the former io Products team led by Jony Ive). The complaint also references a laptop-theft allegation. Apple is seeking damages and an injunction. OpenAI has not yet filed a public response.

Why it matters: Atlas dies less than a year after launch, with OpenAI folding its agentic-browsing features into the ChatGPT desktop app and a Chrome extension — a tacit admission that the browser was a feature, not a destination, and a reminder that even well-funded agent products can get cut fast if adoption doesn't stick.

Supply Chain Security11 Jul

Trojanized npm package (jscrambler) caught harvesting credentials specifically from Claude Desktop, Cursor, Windsurf, VS Code, and Zed

Why it matters: AI coding-tool config and API keys are now a named target class for supply-chain malware, and the attacker adapted in real time across five package versions to defeat each new mitigation — audit any recent jscrambler installs and rotate exposed secrets today.

Detail & sources

The official jscrambler npm CLI package was compromised across five successive versions (8.14.0 through 8.20.0) on 11 July. The attacker first used a preinstall hook, then a bypass of pnpm's allowScripts protection, then a require()-time injection that defeats even --ignore-scripts entirely. The Rust-based infostealer specifically harvests configuration files, API keys, and MCP credentials from Claude Desktop, Cursor, Windsurf, VS Code, and Zed, alongside cloud, crypto-wallet, and password-manager secrets.

Embedded Security10 Jul

Six new U-Boot bootloader flaws undermine secure-boot assumptions on embedded and edge devices

Why it matters: two of the six bugs enable pre-OS code execution on a bootloader used across a huge swath of embedded, IoT, and edge hardware, and the vulnerable code dates back over a decade — any org shipping embedded or edge firmware should check its U-Boot version today.

Detail & sources

Six flaws (BRLY-2026-037 through -042) disclosed 10 July affect U-Boot's FIT image and signature-verification handling. Two — a null-pointer-dereference-turned-buffer-overflow and a stack buffer underflow — enable pre-OS code execution; the rest cause denial of service. The vulnerable code traces back to U-Boot 2013.07 and spans more than 50 releases.
