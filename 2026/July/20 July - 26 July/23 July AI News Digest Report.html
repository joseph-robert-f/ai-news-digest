AMD × Anthropic22 Jul

AMD to invest up to $5B in Anthropic in a 2-gigawatt chip supply deal

Why it matters: this is the clearest crack yet in Nvidia's grip on frontier-lab compute — Anthropic, which has leaned on Google TPUs and Amazon Trainium to diversify away from Nvidia, is now committing multi-year capacity to AMD's Instinct line, while AMD gets a marquee customer and equity upside tied to Anthropic actually scaling on its hardware rather than a blank check.

Detail & sources

AMD said it will make an equity investment of up to $5 billion in Anthropic as the company hits agreed deployment milestones, while Anthropic commits to deploying as much as 2 gigawatts of AMD's next-generation Instinct MI450 accelerators beginning in the first half of 2027 — a chip order industry estimates put in the tens of billions of dollars. The companies also plan to jointly optimize software and hardware, using Claude models to inform AMD's chip and systems design. The deal follows Anthropic's earlier moves to broaden its compute base beyond Nvidia GPUs, and gives AMD a flagship AI-lab customer to set against Nvidia's dominant share of frontier training and inference workloads.

White House × Research21–22 Jul

OSTP memo redirects $200B in federal research funding from universities to AI

Why it matters: this is the biggest structural shift in how the US funds science in decades — shifting money from institutions to individual researchers and "AI-driven research" changes who frontier labs and AI-for-science tool vendors need to court, and puts a 90-day clock on agencies that collectively control tens of billions in R&D spend.

Detail & sources

A White House Office of Science and Technology Policy report titled "Science: A New Golden Age," co-signed by OSTP Director Michael Kratsios and OMB Director Russell Vought, directs federal agencies to shift roughly $200 billion in annual R&D funding away from established universities toward individual scientists, agile ARPA-style organizations, and AI-driven research methods. The plan instructs agencies to supplement consensus peer review with faster mechanisms — "golden tickets," fast grants, and portable awards that follow the researcher rather than the institution — and requires every federal agency with at least $3 billion in FY2026 R&D authority to submit an implementation plan within 90 days. The administration frames the overhaul as necessary to keep pace with China across AI, quantum computing, and fusion research.

Vulnerability × CISA22 Jul

Check Point patches actively exploited SmartConsole auth-bypass flaw

Why it matters: this hits security-management infrastructure itself — an attacker who gets the admin token can rewrite the firewall policy protecting everything behind it — and CISA's compressed patch window signals real observed exploitation, not a theoretical risk; any org running exposed SmartConsole or Multi-Domain Security Management access should treat this as urgent regardless of the federal deadline.

Detail & sources

Check Point disclosed and patched CVE-2026-16232, an authentication-bypass vulnerability (CVSS 9.3) in SmartConsole that lets an unauthenticated attacker obtain an application login token carrying administrator privileges on a Security Management Server or Multi-Domain Security Management server. Exploitation requires the management server to be reachable from the internet with no restrictions on trusted GUI clients. Check Point says it is aware of exploitation affecting a small number of customers; CISA has added the flaw to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, requiring federal civilian agencies to remediate by July 25, 2026. The disclosure lands alongside separately confirmed in-the-wild exploitation of a SharePoint elevation-of-privilege flaw (CVE-2026-56164) and an Active Directory Federation Services flaw (CVE-2026-56155) in this month's patch cycle.

Detail & sources

Glow's platform uses its own specialized AI agents to monitor and control software, third-party AI agents, and developer tools running on employee devices, aiming to stop risky components from entering the corporate environment in the first place rather than remediating after the fact.
