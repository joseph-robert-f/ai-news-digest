Models30 Sep

Google unveils Gemini 4 Argon, but only cyber defenders get it first

Why it matters: Google says it retakes the frontier lead, yet access-gating a cyber-capable model sets a template for how top labs may release from here.

Detail & sources

Argon has a 1M-token output limit (up from 64K) and targets long-horizon coding, knowledge work and cyber defense. It rolls out first to vetted defenders through Google's Fairwind Program, then to paid API tiers and Google AI Ultra. Trusted defenders and Google teams get it “without cyber guardrails.” Reported introductory pricing is $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens. The benchmark-lead claim comes from Google and press coverage of its materials; independent evaluation is still pending.

Security1 Oct

Microsoft: attackers now weaponize vulnerabilities in under 24 hours

Why it matters: Enterprise patch cycles of 30–60 days no longer match attacker speed, so patching and testing pipelines need automation.

Detail & sources

Microsoft's 2026 Digital Defense Report says AI has shifted the near-term advantage to attackers. Post-compromise steps such as credential discovery and lateral movement shrink from days to minutes. In controlled tests, frontier models chained 32 attack steps to full domain compromise of an emulated network without human direction. CVE counts are on track for a record of about 72,000 in 2026.

Kevin Mandia's Armadin raises $255.5M at a $2.5B+ valuation

Why it matters: Investors are betting that defense will be sold as agent swarms, the same shape as the attacks Microsoft describes.

Enterprise1 Oct

OpenAI and Synopsys to build GPT-Synopsys for chip design

Why it matters: A model that drives licensed design tools directly could shorten chip-design cycles and shows how labs will tie models to incumbent software.

Detail & sources

The multi-year deal has OpenAI license Synopsys EDA tools so the model can run design workflows, read tool output and iterate toward power, performance, area, timing and verification goals. Engineers set objectives and review results. The service will run on OpenAI-hosted infrastructure with joint go-to-market and revenue sharing. Synopsys says early engagements with chip customers are under way.

Microsoft rebuilds Copilot around Code and an always-on Autopilot agent

Why it matters: Microsoft is positioning Copilot as a governed digital coworker with its own directory identity, which may suit IT teams wary of unmanaged agents.

Detail & sources

New in this update: Home, Code and Autopilot. Code turns a written description into an app, dashboard or automation in a sandbox that firms can host in their own Microsoft 365 environment. Autopilot monitors channels, follows up on threads and resumes work days later, under permissions admins control. Home and Code roll out through the Frontier program in coming weeks; Autopilot expands to private preview at the end of September. This is a follow-up on earlier-week coverage of workplace agents.
